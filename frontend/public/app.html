FAQ

Hikari is a native, autonomous asset management and multi-strategy yield routing protocol built natively for the Stellar network and Soroban smart contract runtime. Hikari provides tokenized liquid staking (hXLM), dynamic rebalancing keepers, AI-assisted telemetry, and cryptographic proofs of solvency.
When you deposit native XLM into Hikari, the protocol mints liquid yield-bearing hXLM tokens aligned with ERC-4626 share-accounting standards. The underlying capital is routed into verified Soroban DeFi primitives (such as Blend money markets, Phoenix CLAMM yield pools, and atomic MEV backruns). Accrued yields are automatically harvested and compounded by autonomous keepers, raising the redeemable value of each hXLM over time.
Hikari employs formal invariant checks, ERC-4626 dead-share offsets to eliminate first-depositor inflation attacks, autonomous Safety Sentinel circuit breakers that detect rapid pool depegs or oracle divergences, a mandatory 15% liquid reserve floor, and a daily Zero-Knowledge Merkle Proof of Solvency verifying that 100% of user liabilities are backed by on-chain reserves.
Engaging with decentralized finance involves smart contract risks across integrated lending protocols (Blend) and AMMs (Phoenix), market volatility, and potential epoch queue delays during extreme market unbonding conditions. Hikari mitigates these through Bunker Mode and autonomous circuit breakers.
Because Stellar uses the Stellar Consensus Protocol (SCP) without native inflationary Proof-of-Stake rewards, Hikari's dynamic APY (currently 12.4%) is generated exclusively through real, productive on-chain DeFi utility: collateral borrow interest on Blend and dynamic trading fees from Phoenix CLAMM liquidity pools.
A 5% protocol fee is deducted strictly from earned yield (never from your principal). Half of this fee funds the automated 24/7 harvest keeper network for continuous gasless compounding, and the other half builds the protocol reserve treasury.
hXLM is a Stellar Asset Contract (SEP-41) token that represents your claim on the Hikari pool. It is fully transferable, composable, and increases in redemption value against XLM as yield accumulates.
You can mint hXLM directly on this page by connecting your Stellar wallet and depositing native XLM, or swap for it on Stellar DEXes (Soroswap, Phoenix).
hXLM can be supplied as collateral on Blend to borrow stablecoins (USDC), paired in AMM liquidity pools for additional LP trading fees, or wrapped into whXLM for cross-protocol composability.
You can convert hXLM back to XLM through the Withdrawals tab via the native unbonding queue, or execute an instant swap on decentralized exchanges like Soroswap or Phoenix CLAMM in seconds.
Simply click the "Withdrawals" tab at the top of the app. Choose between "Use Hikari" (0% fee, 1-3 days unbonding queue) or "Use DEX" (instant swap in ~10 seconds with market liquidity).

Formal Invariants Specification

Hikari Protocol guarantees 4 mathematical invariants enforced by Soroban smart contracts:

  • Invariant 1: Solvency Conservation: TotalAssets = IdleBuffer + Sum(StrategyAllocations)
  • Invariant 2: Inflation Immunity: Virtual shares and assets prevent first-depositor share price manipulation.
  • Invariant 3: Reserve Floor: Liquid idle reserve must never drop below 15% during standard market regimes.
  • Invariant 4: Max Drawdown Ceiling: GateSeal activates if NAV declines > 15% across a 24h rolling window.

What is hXLM?

What is Bunker Mode?
